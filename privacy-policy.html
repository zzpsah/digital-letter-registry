Privacy Policy

Digital Letter Registry (DLR)

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Digital Letter Registry is a private document-management and search service intended to help its authorized operator preserve, organize, process, and retrieve official school and government letters. Access is restricted to authorized users; it is not a public document-sharing service.

1. Information handled

Depending on the features enabled in a deployment, DLR may handle:

  • Account and authentication information needed to control access.
  • Documents deliberately uploaded or forwarded for archiving, including PDFs, images, and their text.
  • Derived document information such as filenames, summaries, dates, issuing authority, categories, search terms, OCR output, and processing status.
  • Technical and operational logs needed to secure, troubleshoot, and maintain the service.
  • Google Drive authorization and file references used to access the configured archive.

Documents may contain personal or confidential information because they can be official school or government records. Users should submit only documents they are authorized to handle.

2. How information is used

Information is used to authenticate authorized users; preserve originals; extract text from documents; create searchable metadata; answer document-search requests; display or retrieve the corresponding original; prevent abuse; and maintain service reliability and security.

3. Google Drive and connected services

When Google Drive integration is enabled, DLR uses the Google account authorized by the archive operator to perform configured Drive operations, such as storing or retrieving archive files. Google handles authentication and API access under its own terms and privacy policy. DLR does not request a user's Google password.

Depending on the deployment configuration, DLR may also use database, hosting, OCR, or AI-processing providers. If cloud OCR or AI processing is enabled, document content or extracted text needed for that feature may be sent to the configured provider to perform the requested processing. The operator should review the provider's terms, data handling, and configuration before enabling such processing for sensitive records.

If WhatsApp intake is configured, messages or attachments explicitly routed to the DLR intake workflow may be processed for archiving. The integration should be configured only for authorized sources and intended documents.

4. Disclosure and sale

DLR is designed for private, access-controlled use. The service operator does not sell archive documents or personal information. Information may be processed by service providers required to operate features that the operator has enabled, or disclosed when required by applicable law or to protect the service and its users. Access should be limited to authorized users and providers necessary for the configured service.

5. Storage, security, and retention

Original documents are intended to remain in private storage, with access controls protecting the archive. Derived text and metadata may be stored separately to support search. Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards are used, but no internet-connected service can guarantee absolute security.

Records are retained according to the archive operator's needs, applicable obligations, and configured storage policies. Deletion from DLR may not automatically delete a separate original, backup, message, or copy held by Google Drive or another provider; those locations may require separate action. Do not assume a record has been erased from every system unless each relevant location has been checked.

6. Your choices and privacy requests

Only authorized users should access the archive. To ask a question about this policy, request access correction, or raise a privacy concern, contact the service operator at umvtetahali@gmail.com. Please do not email confidential documents, passwords, access tokens, or other secrets. Requests may require verification that the requester is authorized to act on the information.

7. Children

DLR is an administrative document tool, not a service intended for children to use directly. Documents may incidentally contain information about students; access to those records should be limited to people with an authorized educational or administrative purpose.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when DLR features, providers, or practices change. The date at the top indicates the latest revision published here.

9. Contact

Privacy contact: umvtetahali@gmail.com
