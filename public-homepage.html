Keep important school letters easy to find.

UMV Storage Automation (eLetters) is a document registry for authorized school staff to preserve, organize, and search official school and government letters, circulars, orders, PDFs, and scanned images.

What the service does

Preserve originals

Keep submitted documents in the configured private archive so staff can return to the source record.

Organize records

Use document titles, dates, issuing authorities, categories, and related details to make records easier to manage.

Search by context

Find documents using words or remembered topics instead of relying only on the original filename.

Privacy and access

eLetters is intended for private, access-controlled administrative use, not public sharing of school records. Depending on the enabled configuration, authorized workflows may use Google Drive for file storage and may process document text with OCR or an AI provider to support search and organization. Please review the Privacy Policy for details.

Authorized use only. Documents may contain personal or confidential information. Users should submit only records they are authorized to handle, and archive access should be limited to approved users.
