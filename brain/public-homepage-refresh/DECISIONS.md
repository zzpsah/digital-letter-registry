# Decisions

- Keep the page short and plain-language.
- Preserve the purple brand identity and responsive mobile layout.
- Avoid gradients, illustrations, heavy hero graphics, and dashboard-style clutter.
- Use **eLetters** as the visible product name and **Official Letter Registry** as its plain-language descriptor. Do not display the school/storage project label as the brand.
- Make the main action sign-in; keep Privacy Policy available in the main actions and footer.
- State the product objective in one short sentence. Do not add technical details, implementation explanations, or a feature/how-it-works section. Avoid promising public access or absolute security.
