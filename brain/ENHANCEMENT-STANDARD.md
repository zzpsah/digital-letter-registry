# Enhancement Brain Standard

Every substantial feature, integration, migration, repair, or operational enhancement must have `brain/<enhancement>/` containing `PROJECT.md`, `CURRENT_STATE.md`, `ARCHITECTURE.md`, `DECISIONS.md`, `TASKS.md`, `TESTS.md`, `SECURITY.md`, and `HANDOFF.md`. Create it when work begins; update it after meaningful changes; distinguish observed, inferred, planned, blocked, and unverified claims. Documentation is not authorization.
